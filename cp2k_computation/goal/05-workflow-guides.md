> **Preparation of PVA-CBA film**  
> PVA (10 wt %) was dissolved in DMSO at 90 °C under constant stirring. After ultrasonically dispersing CBA in DMSO to reach a CBA/PVA repeat-unit molar ratio of 0.30, 5 mL of 1 M HCl was added dropwise to the mixture at 85 °C, and the reaction was allowed to proceed overnight to graft CBA onto PVA via acetal formation. The film was peeled off and further vacuum-dried (70 °C, 6 h) to remove residual solvent.

> **Synthesis and delamination of Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene**  
> Ti₃C₂Tₓ nanosheets were prepared by selectively etching Al from Ti₃AlC₂ using the MILD route. Briefly, LiF (0.80 g) was dissolved in 9 M HCl (10 mL). Ti₃AlC₂ powder (0.50 g) was slowly added while stirring, and the sealed Teflon vessel was kept at 35 °C for 24 h. The acidic suspension was washed repeatedly with deionized water by centrifugation (3500 rpm, 5 min) until pH ≈ 6. The sediment was redispersed in water (~2 mg mL⁻¹) and sonicated (ice bath, 150 W, 1 h) under Ar to obtain delaminated Ti₃C₂Tₓ flakes, which were collected after 3500 rpm centrifugation (30 min).
